I'm Suzu, a certified native teacher. I know what it's like to study a language for years before it finally clicks, and that's exactly why I teach the way I do.

My path here wasn't a straight one. I'd trained to become a teacher, but what I really wanted was to live abroad. So at university I spent a year volunteering as a Japanese-language assistant in Australian public schools. It was there, watching a student's face light up the moment my words finally "got through," that I fell in love with teaching. I opened my online Japanese school that same year.

It's all built on one belief: everyone has strengths worth building on. So we go beyond textbooks, blending grammar with real conversation and the culture behind every word, until the language finally clicks and starts to feel like your own.

FAQ / よくある質問

Everything you're wondering.質問と答え

What's a lesson actually like?レッスンの流れは？+
Relaxed, friendly and focused on you. We mix conversation, clear explanations and gentle practice, then I send personalized notes and homework so every session moves you forward.
How long is each lesson?レッスンの長さは？+
Each private lesson is 50 minutes, the sweet spot for staying focused and absorbing a lot. Prefer to go deeper? 80-minute sessions are available on request.
I'm a complete beginner. Is that okay?初心者でも大丈夫？+
More than okay. It's my favorite place to start. Most of my students began at zero, with no hiragana and no experience. We take it step by step, and you'll surprise yourself.
What platform do you use?どのツールを使う？+
We meet online via Zoom. I share materials right on screen, and you'll get a recap with notes after every lesson.
Do you teach across time zones?時差は大丈夫？+
Absolutely. I teach students all over the world with flexible slots throughout the day (Japan time), so we'll easily find a time that works for you.
How do I pay, and is the trial really free?支払いと無料体験について+
Yes, your first trial lesson is genuinely free, with no card needed. After that, lessons and packs are paid securely in advance via Stripe (JPY). Simple and stress-free.
